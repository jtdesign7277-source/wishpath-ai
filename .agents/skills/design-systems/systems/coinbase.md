# Design System: Coinbase

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Clean, trustworthy crypto platform aesthetic. Coinbase Blue (#0052ff) as singular brand accent against white and near-black surfaces. Four proprietary fonts: CoinbaseDisplay (hero headlines), CoinbaseSans (UI text), CoinbaseText (body), CoinbaseIcons (icons).

- Coinbase Blue (#0052ff) singular brand accent
- 56px radius pill buttons with blue hover transition
- Near-black (#0a0b0d) dark sections + white light sections
- 1.00 line-height on display headings — ultra-tight
- Cool gray secondary surface (#eef0f3) with blue tint

## 4. Components

**Primary Pill Button**: bg #eef0f3 or #282b31, radius 56px, border 1px solid matching, hover #578bfa
**Blue Bordered**: border 1px solid #0052ff, bg transparent
**Cards**: radius 8px-40px, borders 1px solid rgba(91,97,110,0.2)

## 5. Spacing

Base: 8px. Scale: 4px, 8px, 12px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px
Radius scale: 4-8px (small), 12-16px (standard), 24-32px (large), 40px (XL), 56px (pill)
Breakpoints: 400px, 576px, 640px, 768px, 896px, 1280px, 1440px, 1600px
